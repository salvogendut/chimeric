Back from Japan

2026-11-01

I returned from Japan just two days ago and haven’t quite recovered from the jet lag yet, but I’ve already begun working on some ambitious, almost quixotic projects with my trusty digital angel. I’m still firmly in the realm of retrocomputing, though this time I’ve returned to my beloved Unix workstations. At the same time, a few other micro-projects—centered around the CPC and MSX—are also taking shape. By the way, while I didn’t manage to pick up any MSX hardware in Japan, I did find an MSX cartridge for a pachinko game—made by Casio, no less! I also bought two broken Nintendo Super Famicom consoles. Well, they do power on, but one shows absolutely no signs of life, while the other produces audio but no video. I’m currently waiting to get the right tools to open them up and run some tests. We’ll see what happens!
