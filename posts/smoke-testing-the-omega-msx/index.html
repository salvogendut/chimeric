Smoke testing the Omega MSX

2026-08-19
#MSX  #Omega MSX  #repair  #retrocomputing  #hardware 

After some initial setbacks, the Omega MSX is finally showing signs of life.

The first problem was a defective Yamaha 8139 chip, which prevented the system from booting at all. After replacing it, we were able to get the machine displaying C-BIOS and the diagnostics screen.

That led to the next fault: capacitor C64. It should measure 220 µF, but was only measuring 68 µF. This failed capacitor was responsible for a strange yellow ghosting effect on the screen.

There is one small setback left: I had to back-order a replacement RTC chip after accidentally tearing off one of its pins while manoeuvring it back into its socket. Unfortunately, the damage is not repairable.

Once the RTC replacement arrives and is fitted, the Omega should be fairly complete. Then it will be time to try it with some real cartridges and/or games.
