I've been collecting bits and pieces since last year. The computer PCBs were kinda the easy part, I got a good deal (at the time) fro JLCPCB and the prices were still low. I got the majority of the chips from some dude on Ebay who sells an 'Omega kit (sans the V9958), got the V9958 from Aliexpress (I guess I was lucky, I got 1 working V9958 out of 3 attempts, the other two were fakes). And finally the case was downloaded from Thingiverse and 3D printed by yours faithful JLCPCB. I think the spending is around 350 euros without my soldering work. And finally it is done (though I still need to attach a reset button and a ROM bank switcher. But here it is. Just mind the cabling and the order of assembly, also if you, like me , have a memory expansion you are going to be using only half keyboard holder. See pics
