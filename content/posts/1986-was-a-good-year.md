I was 13 in 1986. That year was epic for me, for several reasons. I had just passed the exam to jump from junior high school to senior high school, Summer was being sweet to me , spending the holidays at my Aunt's place. Uncle Nino and Auntie Borina were absolutely the best, they would let me play outside and do all sorts of mischief (I even risked incinerating an entire field near the house one day) and in the evening they would always have friends and neighbors coming and spending time on that beautiful terrace in their villa near Riesi. One day Uncle and Dad came back from a trip and gave me one hell of a gift for my promotion: a Commodore 128. That was, for me, the beginning of everything. Thanks to a friend of my Aunt's: Professor Lo Giudice, I learned BASIC and a bit of assembly there, started programming , started loving machines and that love survived until now. Commodore 128 was a bit of a crazy mash-up of everything, had a 128 mode with a very powerful BASIC, full compatibility with C64 and its games (in C64 mode) and a third more serious mode called CP/M which really puzzled me and that I had no idea how to try (I only had a tape and no disk drives). Long story short, 40 years have passed and now I have decided to give C128 a little extra love, for the sake of memories, for the sake of the old days. So 1986 was born, an emulator which broadly borrows from VICE (Thank you guys, I don't think it would have been possible to create a Commodore 128 emulator from scratch without your guidance), but unlinke VICE it does not emulate the built-in C64 mode. As far as I can I want 1986 to have nothing to do with C64. C128 was a neglected machine because of its more popular sibiling. I want the C128 to be itself 100% and only run software that it is meant for it. Even if there isn't and I have to write it. [Try it](https://github.com/salvogendut/1986/) if you want, it's still buggy as hell and remember I did not write this for you. I wrote it for me. And if I close my eyes a bit I can still see those beautiful Summer sunsets and be a kid once more again.
